The real test shows pictures. Here the signs are described in words, so use
this page beside the official chart (付表3 of the 教則) or a sign poster.

**Signs** (標識) are **main signs** (本標識) plus **auxiliary signs**
(補助標識) hung beneath them to narrow the rule: hours, days, vehicle types,
start and end, the reason.

| Family | What it does | Usual look |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulatory** 規制標識 | forbids, or orders a way of driving | red rim on white, or white on blue |
| **Indication** 指示標識 | says something is allowed, or marks a place | white on blue |
| **Warning** 警戒標識 | danger ahead | yellow diamond, black symbol |
| **Guide** 案内標識 | places, directions, distances | blue (green on expressways) |

**Look-alikes.**

- **Road closed** (通行止め): closed to everyone - pedestrians, vehicles,
  trams. **Closed to vehicles** (車両通行止め): vehicles only.
- **No entry** (車両進入禁止): red disc with a white bar. It stands at the
  **exit of a one-way street**: you may not enter from this end.
- **No parking** (駐車禁止): blue disc, red rim, **one** red slash.
  **No stopping or parking** (駐停車禁止): **two** slashes, crossed. A figure
  such as **8-20** at the top means 8:00 to 20:00.
- **No crossing to the right-hand side to overtake**
  (追越しのための右側部分はみ出し通行禁止): overtaking is still allowed if you
  stay on your own side. With the auxiliary sign **追越し禁止** beneath, it
  becomes **no overtaking** at all.
- **Maximum speed** (最高速度): a plain figure. **Minimum speed** (最低速度):
  the figure is **underlined**. A maximum of 50 does not lift the **30 km/h**
  limit for ordinary mopeds.
- **Stop** (一時停止): red inverted triangle, 止まれ. **Slow** (徐行): white
  inverted triangle, red rim.
- **Sound horn** (警笛鳴らせ): at that spot. **Horn zone** (警笛区間): within
  the zone, at blind junctions, blind bends and blind hill tops.
- **Priority road** (優先道路) is an indication sign - you are on it.
  **Priority road ahead** (前方優先道路) is a regulatory sign - the road you
  are about to meet has priority.
- **Motor vehicles only** (自動車専用): expressways and motorways.
  **Pedestrians only** (歩行者等専用): a pedestrian road.

**Height, width, weight** (高さ制限, 最大幅, 重量制限): the height and width
count the **load** too; the weight is the **gross** weight.

**Road markings** (標示) are **regulatory markings** (規制標示) and
**indication markings** (指示標示).

- **Centre line.** Yellow solid: no crossing to the right to overtake. White
  solid: don't cross. White broken: you may cross to overtake when it is safe.
- **Lane lines.** **Yellow** between lanes: no lane changes. Where a yellow
  line runs beside a white one, the ban applies to the lane **on the yellow
  side**.
- **Kerb.** Yellow **solid**: no stopping or parking. Yellow **broken**: no
  parking.
- **No-entry area** (立入り禁止部分): yellow outline, white hatching - keep
  out. **Keep-clear box** (停止禁止部分): white hatching - don't enter if you
  would have to stop inside it. **Channelling strip** (導流帯): white stripes
  guiding traffic - not for driving on.
- **Safety zone** (安全地帯): yellow outer frame, white inner frame.
- A white **diamond** on the road: crosswalk or bicycle crossing ahead. A
  white **inverted triangle**: priority road ahead.
- **Stop line** (停止線): where to stop. **Two-stage stop line**
  (二段停止線): motorcycles at the front line, cars at the rear one.
