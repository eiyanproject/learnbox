## Chapter 0: the words in this lesson

Every term, command and function this lesson uses, with what it means. Read
it first, or come back whenever a word is unfamiliar; each one is explained
again, with examples, where the lesson uses it.

**Keep to the left of the centre** (中央線から左の部分). You may cross to the right
only when the road is one-way; when roadworks leave too little room on the
left (keep the crossing as small as possible); to overtake on a clear road whose
left side is **under 6 m wide** - unless signs or markings forbid it; or on a
steep bend marked "keep right" (右側通行).

**Lanes** (車両通行帯). With no lanes, keep to the left of the road. With two
lanes your way, use the left one. With three or more, leave the rightmost lane
for overtaking - slower traffic to the left - and come back out of it as soon
as you have passed. Don't straddle two lanes or change lanes without need.

**Never drive on**: pavements, side strips (路側帯) or cycle tracks - except to
cross them into a property; safety zones (安全地帯) or "no entry" markings; the
**road shoulder** (路肩, 0.5 m from the edge) on a road with no pavement or side
strip; or tram tracks (軌道敷), except where a sign allows or to turn right.

**Emergency vehicles** (緊急自動車). Near an intersection: get out of the
intersection, pull to the left and **stop**. Elsewhere: pull to the left and
let it pass. On a one-way road where keeping left would block it, pull right.

**Route buses** (路線バス). Don't block a bus signalling to leave a stop - unless
that would take sudden braking or swerving. Most cars may not use a **bus-only
lane** (専用通行帯), except to turn. In a **bus-priority lane** (優先通行帯) you
must leave it promptly when a bus comes, and not enter it at all if traffic is
too heavy to get out.

**Marked drivers** (初心者マーク and others). Unless avoiding danger, don't
squeeze alongside (幅寄せ) or cut in front of a car showing the beginner mark
(licence under a year), the senior mark (driver 70 or over), the hearing- or
physically-impaired mark, or a provisional-licence learner.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第5章第2節 and 第3節8.
