**Look at the signal in front of you** (前方の信号). A red light to the side does
not mean yours is green: some signals turn red in every direction at once, and
some change at different times for different directions (時差式信号機).

| Signal | For a car |
|---|---|
| Green (青色の灯火) | Go straight, left or right. |
| Yellow (黄色の灯火) | Do not go past the stop position - unless you were so close when it changed that you cannot stop safely. |
| Red (赤色の灯火) | Do not go past the stop position. A car already turning left, or right, in the intersection may finish the turn; turning right, it must not get in the way of traffic going on green. |
| Green arrow (青色の灯火の矢印) | Go the arrow's way even if the light is yellow or red. A right arrow also allows a U-turn. |
| Yellow arrow (黄色の灯火の矢印) | For trams only. Cars may not go. |
| Flashing yellow (黄色の灯火の点滅) | Go, watching other traffic. |
| Flashing red (赤色の灯火の点滅) | Stop at the stop position, then go. |

**The stop position** (停止位置) is just before the stop line. With no line: just
before the intersection - or before a crosswalk or bicycle crossing right at it.

**Left turn permitted** (左折可): a white board with a blue left arrow, at the
road's left edge or on the signal. You may turn left on yellow or red, without
getting in the way of pedestrians and cyclists crossing on their signal.

**Police officers and traffic wardens** (警察官・交通巡視員). Their hand signals
and light signals **come first, even when they differ from the traffic light**.

- Arm held out sideways (or lowered without turning): traffic moving along the
  line of the arm, parallel to the officer's front, has a green; traffic facing
  the officer's front or back has a red.
- Arm raised straight up: the parallel traffic has a yellow; the crossing
  traffic has a red.
- Away from intersections, crosswalks and railway crossings, stop 1 m before
  the officer.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第1章第2節・第3節 and 付表1.
