title: Where you may not stop or park
summary: What counts as parking, the places where both are banned, the places where only parking is banned, and the distances that go with each.
order: 1
kind: quiz
---

**Parking** (駐車): the car stays stopped, or the driver is away and can't drive
it off at once. **Not** parking: stopping for people to get in or out, or to
load or unload for **5 minutes or less**. **Stopping** (停車) is any short stop
that isn't parking.

**No stopping, no parking** (駐停車禁止) - except for a red light or to avoid
danger:

| Place | Distance |
|---|---|
| "No stopping or parking" signs and markings | - |
| On tram tracks | - |
| Near the top of a slope, and on a steep slope (up **or** down) | - |
| In a tunnel | - |
| Intersections | within **5 m** of the edge |
| Bends | within **5 m** |
| Crosswalks and bicycle crossings | **5 m** before and after |
| Railway crossings | **10 m** before and after |
| Safety zones | their left side, and **10 m** before and after |
| Bus and tram stop signs | within **10 m**, during operating hours only |

**No parking** (駐車禁止) - stopping is still allowed:

| Place | Distance |
|---|---|
| "No parking" signs and markings | - |
| Fire alarms (火災報知機) | within **1 m** |
| Entrances to car parks and garages | within **3 m** |
| The edge of roadworks | within **5 m** |
| Fire-fighting equipment stores and water tanks, and their road entrances | within **5 m** |
| Fire hydrants, fire-water signs and water tank intakes | within **5 m** |

**Leave 3.5 m.** Don't park where less than **3.5 m** of road would remain to
the car's right (or the distance a sign states) - unless you are loading and can
drive off at once, or helping someone injured.

**How**: along the left edge of the road, or of the carriageway where there is
a pavement or side strip. A wide side strip may be entered if you leave
**0.75 m** - but never one marked with a solid-and-broken white line or two
white lines. Never double-park alongside a parked car.

**The road is not a garage.** A car's storage place must be off the road and
within **2 km** of where it is based. Don't park in the same spot on a road for
**12 hours** or more - **8 hours** at night.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第5章第8節1-3・9.
