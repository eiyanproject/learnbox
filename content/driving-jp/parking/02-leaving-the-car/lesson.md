**Time-limited parking zones** (時間制限駐車区間). Start a **parking meter** at
once. With a **ticket machine**, get the ticket at once and display it at the
front of the car - inside the windscreen - where it can be read from the front.
Don't stay past the time on the sign.

**Reserved spaces** (高齢運転者等専用場所): only for cars showing the permit
(専用場所駐車標章), which goes to drivers aged **70 or over**, drivers with a
hearing or physical condition on their licence, and drivers who are **pregnant
or within 8 weeks of giving birth**.

**Illegally parked**: move the car at once if an officer tells you to. If nobody
is there to be told, it can be towed - at the cost of the driver, user or
owner. A confirmation sticker (放置車両確認標章) may be fixed to it; don't tear
or deface it, though the car's user or driver may take it off, and should
before driving.

**Leaving the car** - against it rolling away:

- engine off, **handbrake on**;
- gear in **reverse on the flat or facing downhill**, **low (first) facing
  uphill**; in an automatic, **P**;
- on a slope, **chock a wheel**.

Against theft: engine off and the key with you; windows shut and doors locked;
steering lock on; valuables locked in the boot.

**Automatics** (オートマチック車).

- **Starting**: handbrake on, lever in **P**, foot on the brake, then start.
- **Setting off**: brake pedal firmly down, select D (or R) and look to check
  it, release the handbrake, ease off the brake, then gently accelerate.
- **Creep** (クリープ現象): with a gear engaged the car moves by itself, so keep
  the brake pedal down when stopped; handbrake too. For a long wait, use **N**.
- The engine revs higher just after starting and with the air conditioning on,
  so hold the brake especially firmly then.
- **Parking**: brake pedal down, handbrake on, **then** P - and only once the
  car has fully stopped.

Driver-assistance systems (ASV: automatic braking, adaptive cruise) have
limits; they support a driver who is still watching and driving.
