**Expressways** (高速道路) are national expressways (高速自動車国道) and
motorways (自動車専用道路).

**Who may not use them**: mini-cars (ミニカー), small motorcycles of 125 cc or
less (小型二輪車) and ordinary mopeds. On national expressways, also vehicles
that cannot do **50 km/h** by construction, or because of what they tow.

**Before joining**: fuel, coolant, engine oil, fan belt, tyre pressure (a
little **higher** than usual for expressway speeds), tread depth; the load
secured; a warning triangle or light ready; rests planned.

**Joining.** Use the **acceleration lane** and get up to speed. You must not
hinder traffic already on the **main carriageway** (本線車道 - the part for
normal high-speed driving, without the acceleration, deceleration and climbing
lanes, side strip and shoulder).

**Speed** on a national expressway main carriageway, where no sign says
otherwise:

| Vehicle | Maximum | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary cars, large passenger vehicles, motorcycles | 100 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Large goods vehicles, specified medium goods vehicles | 90 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Others (three-wheelers, towing vehicles) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |

Where the two directions are not physically separated, the table does not
apply: ordinary-road rules do.

**Following distance** on a dry road with good tyres: about **100 m at
100 km/h**, **80 m at 80 km/h**. Wet road and worn tyres: about **double**.
In rain at speed the tyres can ride up on the water and the steering and
brakes stop working - **hydroplaning**.

**On the carriageway.**

- Keep a little to the left of your lane, guided by the left white line.
- No driving on the side strip or shoulder. **No U-turns, no reversing, no
  crossing the central reservation.**
- Slow vehicles use the climbing lane (登坂車線) where there is one.
- Overtaking: signal early; pull back in once the whole of the overtaken car
  shows in the interior mirror.
- Braking: change down for engine braking and press the foot brake in several
  stages. No sudden steering. Slow down in strong wind - especially at tunnel
  exits and cuttings - and before entering a tunnel.
- At night: main beam, except when meeting or following another car.

**Stopping and parking are forbidden**, except: stopping to avoid danger; a
breakdown, on a shoulder or side strip that is wide enough; parking areas and
toll booths.

**If the car cannot go on**: set the **warning triangle or light on the road
behind the car**; at night (or by day with visibility of 200 m or less) add
hazard, parking or tail lights; call 110 and use an emergency telephone; then
**do not stay in the car** - get behind the guard rail.

**Leaving.** Watch the direction signs, move in good time into the lane that
leads to the exit, use the **deceleration lane**, and check the speedometer
rather than trusting your senses.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第7章.
