title: Before you drive

**Check before every drive**: the right licence for the vehicle, **with you**;
a valid inspection certificate (自動車検査証) and compulsory insurance
certificate (自賠責保険証明書) **in the car**; the conditions on your licence met
(glasses, for instance); emergency signalling equipment (a flare) and a warning
triangle or light on board.

**Marks**:

- **Beginner mark** (初心者マーク): **must** be shown, front and back, for the
  first year after getting an ordinary licence.
- **Hearing-impaired mark** (聴覚障害者マーク): **must** be shown by drivers
  whose licence carries that condition.
- **Senior mark** (高齢者マーク), drivers 70 and over, and the
  **physically-impaired mark**: **should** be shown.
- **Provisional-licence learner plate** (仮免許練習標識): front and back when
  practising - with someone beside you who has held the licence for that
  vehicle for **3 years or more**, or holds a second-class licence.

**You must not drive** after drinking (酒気帯び), under the influence of drugs
or solvents, or when **exhausted** (過労). Drink from the night before can still
be affecting you the next morning. Don't drive after medicine that causes
drowsiness, and hold back when ill or worried.

**Plan**: route, time, rests, parking. On a long drive rest at least **once
every 2 hours**; if sleepy, rest before driving on.

**Seat belts**: the driver wears one and must have **every passenger** wear one
- front and back seats, air bags or not - unless illness or the like makes it
unavoidable. Lap belt low across the hips; shoulder belt off the neck and not
slack. Pregnant: wear both lap and shoulder belt, clear of the bump, and ask
your doctor. **Small children** (幼児) must use a child seat suited to their
size.

**Phones and screens**: don't use a mobile phone or stare at a navigation
screen while the car is moving. Silence the phone before driving.

**Doors**: check behind before opening; open a little first, pause, then get
out. On a busy road, get in and out on the **left**. The driver is responsible
for passengers not opening doors carelessly. No clogs (げた) or high heels at
the wheel.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第4章第1節・第2節1(3) and 第5章第1節.
