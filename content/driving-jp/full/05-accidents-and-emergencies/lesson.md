title: Accidents, breakdowns and earthquakes
summary: The three duties after a crash, first aid, towing a broken-down car, and what to do with the car when the ground shakes.

**After a traffic accident** the driver and crew must:

1. **Stop** somewhere safe that does not block traffic (shoulder, open
   ground) and switch off the engine - to prevent a second accident.
2. **Help the injured**: first aid within your ability, such as stopping
   bleeding with gauze or a clean handkerchief, until a doctor or ambulance
   arrives. Don't move an injured person needlessly - especially with a head
   injury - **unless** a further accident threatens; then get them to safety
   quickly.
3. **Report to the police**: where it happened, how many are hurt and how
   badly, what was damaged, what the vehicles were carrying. Then follow the
   officer's instructions.

Even a minor injury is reported. After a hard blow to the head, see a doctor
even with no visible injury: after-effects can appear later.

**Bystanders**: help willingly. If you see a hit-and-run, help the injured and
report the number, type and colour of the car to 110. No smoking at the scene:
there may be spilt fuel.

**Breakdowns.**

- Park where you are not in the way and fix or refuel promptly.
- Ordinary road, at night: hazard lights on, or a warning triangle or light
  out. By day: a triangle, or the boot lid open, shows the car is stopped.
- Expressway: the triangle or light behind the car is compulsory; at night
  add hazard, parking or tail lights.
- Don't leave a broken-down car on the road: have it moved off quickly.
- **Towing with an ordinary car**: a strong rope, a gap of **5 m or less**
  between the cars, and a **white cloth at least 30 cm square** on the rope.

**Earthquakes.**

- **Earthquake early warning** while driving: hazard lights on to warn others,
  no hard braking, slow down gently.
- **A major earthquake** while driving: stop on the **left side** of the road,
  avoiding sudden steering and braking. Listen to the car radio. If you drive
  on, watch for road damage, dead signals and obstacles.
- **Leaving the car to evacuate**: off the road if you can. If it must stay on
  the road: on the left, engine off, **key left in the car** (in the ignition
  or somewhere obvious), **windows shut, doors unlocked** - so that it can be
  moved.
- Don't use a car to evacuate, except when there is no other way to escape a
  tsunami.

**Disaster traffic control** (災害対策基本法): when an area or road is closed
to all but emergency traffic, move your car off the closed road (or off the
road altogether, if a whole area is closed) promptly. If you cannot, park
along the left edge so emergency vehicles can pass. Follow police
instructions.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第10章.
