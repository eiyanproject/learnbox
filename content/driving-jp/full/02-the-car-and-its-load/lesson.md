**What the licence covers.** An **ordinary licence** (普通免許) covers ordinary
cars, small special vehicles (小型特殊自動車) and ordinary mopeds
(一般原動機付自転車) - not motorcycles. Towing another vehicle needs a **towing
licence** (けん引免許) as well, **except** when the towed vehicle's gross weight
is **750 kg or less**, or when towing a broken-down car by rope or crane.

**Checks.**

- **Daily check** (日常点検): the user's own responsibility, at suitable times
  judged from mileage and condition. Business vehicles, private large and
  medium vehicles, goods vehicles and rental cars: **once a day before
  driving**.
- **Periodic inspection** (定期点検): private passenger cars **every year**;
  private goods vehicles and rental passenger cars every **6 months**; business
  vehicles and private large or medium vehicles every **3 months**.
- A car whose steering, brakes or silencer is out of order must not be driven.
- Carry emergency signalling equipment (flare or red lamp); on an expressway,
  a warning triangle or light too.

**People and loads.**

- Nobody rides where there is no seat; nothing is loaded where there is no
  load space. The exceptions: the minimum number of people needed to watch a
  load, or permission from the police chief at the starting point.
- The seating capacity **includes the driver**. Children under 12 count
  **three as two**.
- Ordinary car load: length up to the car's length **x 1.2**, width up to the
  car's width **x 1.2**, height **3.8 m** from the ground (**2.5 m** for
  three-wheelers and cars of 660 cc or less). Overhang: no more than **1/10**
  of the car's length front or back, **1/10** of its width left or right.
- An ordinary moped: one person, **30 kg**.
- A load must not hide the indicators, number plate, brake lights or tail
  lights, or upset the car's balance. If something falls off, remove it
  promptly.

**Eyes.** Sight is the sense that matters most. Vision worsens with speed,
near objects first. Tiredness shows in the eyes before anywhere else. A sudden
change in brightness blinds for a moment: slow down entering and leaving a
tunnel.

**Forces.**

- **Friction** stops the car; on wet asphalt there is less of it and the
  braking distance grows.
- **Centrifugal force** pushes the car to the outside of a bend: greater on a
  tighter bend, and with a high or lopsided load the car tips more easily.
  Brake on the straight, before the bend.
- **Impact**: 60 km/h into a concrete wall is like falling about **14 m** - a
  fifth floor.
- Braking distance, centrifugal force and impact all grow with the **square of
  the speed**: double the speed, **four times** the force.

**Eco-driving**: gentle starts, steady speed, no idling when parked.
