## Chapter 0: the words in this lesson

Every term, command and function this lesson uses, with what it means. Read
it first, or come back whenever a word is unfamiliar; each one is explained
again, with examples, where the lesson uses it.

### Terms

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **safety check** | 安全確認 | looking in the mirrors and around before you move |
| **signal** | 合図 | indicator, brake light or hand signal telling others what you will do |
| **indicator** | 方向指示器 | the flashing turn light |
| **hand signal** | 手による合図 | the arm signals that can replace the lights |
| **brake lights** / **reversing lights** | 制動灯・後退灯 | lit when slowing / reversing |
| **horn** | 警音器 | for danger and "sound horn" places only |
| **sound horn sign** / **horn zone** | 警笛鳴らせ・警笛区間 | at that spot / at blind places within the zone |
| **changing course** | 進路変更 | moving sideways into another position or lane |
| **yellow lane line** | | no lane changes across it |
| **U-turn** | 転回 | turning round to go back |
| **reversing** | 後退 | driving backwards |
| **crawl** | 徐行 | a speed at which you can stop at once |

---

**Check, then signal** (安全確認と合図). Before changing course, turning round
or reversing, check behind with the mirrors, then signal.

| Doing | Signal from | How |
|---|---|---|
| Turning left or right, or a U-turn | **30 m** before the point (or the intersection) | indicator - or by hand |
| Moving left or right while going the same way | about **3 seconds** before | indicator - or by hand |
| Slowing or stopping | when you do it | brake lights - or arm out, angled down |
| Reversing | when you do it | reversing lights - or arm out, angled down, waved back and forth |

Hand signals: for a **left** turn, right arm out with the elbow bent upward, or
left arm out straight; for a **right** turn, right arm out straight, or left arm
with the elbow bent upward. Use a hand signal as well when the setting sun makes
the indicator hard to see. **Stop signalling** promptly when done, and never
signal without reason.

**The horn** (警音器). Sound it where a "sound horn" sign stands (警笛鳴らせ),
and in a "horn zone" (警笛区間) at a blind intersection, bend or hilltop. You
may also use it when unavoidable to avoid danger. Otherwise, don't.

**Changing course** (進路変更). Not without need, and never if the car behind
would have to brake or swerve suddenly. Check mirrors **and** look. Lanes split
by a **yellow** line: don't cross it to change lanes. The same applies to a
white line with a yellow line beside it on your side.

**Crossing, U-turns, reversing**: not where they would get in the way of
pedestrians or other traffic, and not where signs or markings forbid them. To
leave the road to the left, keep well left and crawl; to the right, keep near
the centre (on a one-way road, the right edge) and crawl. Don't block a car
ahead that is signalling to move over to leave the road - unless that needs
sudden braking or swerving.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第5章第5節.
