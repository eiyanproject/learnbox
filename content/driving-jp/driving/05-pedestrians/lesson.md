## Chapter 0: the words in this lesson

Every term, command and function this lesson uses, with what it means. Read
it first, or come back whenever a word is unfamiliar; each one is explained
again, with examples, where the lesson uses it.

### Terms

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **pedestrian** | 歩行者 | a person on foot |
| **safe gap** | 安全な間隔 | enough room beside a person to pass safely |
| **safety zone** | 安全地帯 | a protected island in the road |
| **tram stop** | 停留所 | where people get on and off a tram, often from the road |
| **crosswalk** / **bicycle crossing** | 横断歩道・自転車横断帯 | slow so you can stop; stop if anyone is crossing; no overtaking within 30 m before |
| **white or yellow cane** | 白・黄色のつえ | used by a person who cannot see well |
| **guide dog** | 盲導犬 | a dog leading a person who cannot see |
| **school bus** | 通学通園バス | crawl past it when it is stopped |
| **school route** | 通学路 | where children walk to school |
| **side strip** | 路側帯 | the pedestrian strip at a road's edge; stop before crossing it |
| **cyclist** | 自転車 | a vehicle; overtake with a safe gap or crawl |
| **crawl** | 徐行 | a speed at which you can stop at once |

---

**Passing pedestrians**: leave a safe gap, or crawl. Crawl past a safety zone
with people in it. Behind a tram stopped at a stop, **stop** and wait until
people have got on, off and across - unless nobody is getting on or off and
there is **1.5 m or more** between you and the tram, or there is a safety zone:
then you may crawl past. Crawl through puddles and mud so you don't splash
people.

**Crosswalks and bicycle crossings** (横断歩道・自転車横断帯):

- Approaching one, slow so you **can stop before it**, unless it is clear that
  nobody is crossing.
- If anyone is crossing or about to cross, **stop before it** (before the stop
  line if there is one) and let them go.
- If a car is stopped at or just before one, **stop** before going past it.
- **No overtaking or passing** on one or within **30 m** before it.
- At an intersection without a crosswalk, don't get in the way of people
  crossing.

**Stop or crawl** so they can pass safely for: someone in a wheelchair; a person
with a **white or yellow cane** or a **guide dog**; a child walking alone; an
elderly person with a cane or walker, or who walks with difficulty. Crawl past
a stopped **school bus**. Near schools, kindergartens, playgrounds and
school-route signs, expect children to run out.

**Crossing a pavement or side strip** to reach a property: **stop** just before
it, and don't get in the way of pedestrians.

**Cyclists** are vehicles and normally use the road. Overtaking one, leave a
safe gap or crawl - expect them to wobble. At intersections, watch for cyclists
crossing and for ones on your left as you turn.

Source: 交通の方法に関する教則, 第5章第3節1-7.
